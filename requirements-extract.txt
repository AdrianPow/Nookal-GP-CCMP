# Extraction layer only — the Nookal client itself needs just `requests`.
#
# Deliberately minimal. pdfplumber was used originally but pulls in
# pdfminer.six -> cryptography, which needs a Rust toolchain and fails to
# build on a stock Mac. pypdf extracted every field identically across the
# ten sample referrals, and has no dependencies of its own.
pypdf>=5.0

# Only needed to pull page images out of scanned referrals for OCR. Ships
# as a prebuilt wheel, so nothing compiles.
pillow>=10.0

# Scanned referrals also need the Tesseract binary. Without it — or without
# Pillow — they still reach the review queue flagged for manual entry, and
# digital referrals are unaffected.
